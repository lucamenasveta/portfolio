About Me

I came to video sideways. I spent a few years working in restaurants and bars, which is where I learned to read a room, work a long shift without losing the plot, and talk to people who are busy and distracted. Those turn out to be most of the job on a shoot.

I picked up a camera properly a couple of years ago and have been freelancing since, first in Bangkok and now in Phuket. Fitness and wellness became my lane partly by accident and partly because I train myself, so I know what a real session looks like and I can tell when a shot is lying about it.

How I work

  • One conversation before the shoot about who the video is for and where it will run
  • A shot list, so the day has a spine and we are not improvising at hour four
  • A first cut within a week, then one round of notes included
  • Files delivered in every ratio the client needs, not just the one I edited in

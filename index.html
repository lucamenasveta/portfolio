Welcome

I am a freelance videographer working mostly in fitness and wellness. I shoot, direct and edit short-form video for gyms, coaches and studios, and I handle the whole job from the first call through to the delivered cut.

This site is where the work lives. Have a look at the projects, read a bit about how I work, and get in touch if you have something in mind.

What I Do

Shooting

Single-operator shoots in gyms and studios, built to move fast and stay out of the way of the training.

Editing

Short-form cuts for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, delivered in the aspect ratios each platform actually rewards.

Content Strategy

Planning a shoot day so it produces weeks of posts rather than one hero video and nothing else.
